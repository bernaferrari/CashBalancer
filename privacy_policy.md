# Cash Balancer privacy policy

Updated November 14, 2026.

Cash Balancer stores your portfolios, asset quantities, prices, and preferences on your device or in your browser. The app does not require an account or send your portfolio data or app logs to the developer. Fonts are bundled with the app.

If you export a backup, it includes your portfolio data in plain JSON. You choose where to save it or whom to share it with. Any service you share the file with handles it under its own privacy policy.

You can remove your portfolio data in the app. Clearing the app's storage or the site's browser data also removes local data. It does not remove any backups you saved elsewhere.
